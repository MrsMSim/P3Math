For teachers: syllabus link
  • P3 Measurement – Time: telling time in the 24-hour clock and converting between 12-hour and 24-hour clock.
  • Timeline jumps builds up counting on and back on a timeline: 1 h, 30 min, 15 min and 10 min jumps, jumping to the next o'clock, then mixed jumps. Pupils move up after 5 correct in a stage.
  • Finding start time, end time and duration in hours and minutes (Solve it uses a timeline model as the hint).
  • Adding and subtracting time in hours and minutes; changing hours to minutes and back.
  • Level 1 keeps times in 5-minute steps and in 24-hour form. Level 2 uses any minute, mixes 12-hour and 24-hour, and includes the midnight hour.
  • Progress: pupils pick their class and name, and every finished question is saved on that device. Green = at least 5 done and 80% or more correct. Amber = 50–79% correct, or fewer than 5 done. Red = under 50% correct. Grey = not started. Class view shows all pupils who used the device. To change class lists, edit CLASSES at the top of the script.
  • Answers accept 1-digit hours (9:15) but remind pupils to write 2 digits (09:15).
